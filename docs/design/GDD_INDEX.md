# Scrap Kingdom: A Dead Country — GDD index

The user-provided complete GDD is the authoritative design source for this project. It defines a single-player scrapyard management, business simulation, resource management, and crafting game set in a fictional post-conflict country.

## Design pillars
1. Resourcefulness
2. Discovery
3. Business management
4. Progression
5. Visible development
6. Meaningful choices
7. Accessible complexity

## Major design areas
- World, atmosphere, and narrative approach
- Main isometric yard and building placement
- Facilities and upgrades
- Items, materials, condition, inventory, and storage
- Buying, selling, dynamic markets, and business expenses
- Scavenging locations, expeditions, vehicles, and equipment
- Sorting, dismantling, repair, and manufacturing
- Experimentation, recipe discovery, mastery, and worker expertise
- Workforce, customers, orders, contracts, negotiation, and reputation
- Simulation clock, parallel activities, events, tiers, and progression
- UI, pixel-art direction, animation, sound, saving, mobile, and performance

## Source-of-truth rule
When implementation decisions are made, follow the complete user-supplied GDD. If a detail is absent or ambiguous, record the question rather than silently inventing a rule. The GDD's explicit scope boundaries are binding unless the owner approves a revision.
